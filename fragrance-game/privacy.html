Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Scentle is a free browser game. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. This page explains the little data that does move when you play, and your rights over it.

Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Péter Rácz, Kálti Márk utca 2/b, 1183 Budapest, Hungary, peter.racz.rhr@gmail.com. Write to that address for any question or request about your data.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it works the way you left it. This stays on your device; we never receive it.

WhatWhyHow long
Your settings (difficulty, gender filter, daily or unlimited)To open the game with the same settingsUntil you clear site data
Today's round per settingSo a reload doesn't lose your daily progressUntil you clear site data
Games played, wins and streaksTo show your stats at the end of a roundUntil you clear site data
Recently played bottlesSo unlimited mode doesn't repeat itselfUntil you clear site data
Light or dark themeTo remember your choiceUntil you clear site data

This storage is strictly necessary for features you ask for, so it does not need consent under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings ("clear site data" for this website).

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). To deliver pages and protect the site against attacks, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We use it only for delivery and security, based on our legitimate interest in running a safe website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Cloudflare may process this data outside the EU; see Cloudflare's privacy policy for the safeguards it uses. We do not keep our own visitor logs.

Bottle photos

Bottle photos load from Fragrantica's image server (fimgs.net). When they load, your browser sends that server your IP address and browser type, as with any image on the web. The game tells your browser not to send the address of this page with those requests. Fragrantica's own privacy policy covers what it does with this data.

Copying your result

"Copy result" puts your score grid on your clipboard only when you press it. The game never reads your clipboard.

Links to other sites

The Fragrantica link at the end of a round opens another website, which has its own privacy rules.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, ask us to limit how it is used, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Articles 15 to 21). Because we hold no data about you ourselves, most requests will concern the hosting data above; we will pass them to Cloudflare where needed. Write to peter.racz.rhr@gmail.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Hungary that is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11, naih.hu. You may also contact the authority where you live or work.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new, for example analytics or accounts, this page will be updated before that happens, and the date at the top will change.
