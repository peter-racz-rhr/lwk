How to play

Scentle is a free perfume guessing game. You see a fragrance's notes, the way Fragrantica lists them in top, heart and base notes, and you guess which perfume it is. It's like Wordle for fragrance lovers: one daily puzzle per mode, plus unlimited practice.

Guessing

Type a perfume or brand into the search box and pick it from the list. Each guess is compared with the hidden fragrance on ten clues:

  • Brand, gender and country: green when they match.
  • Year, rating and votes: green for a match, yellow when close, and an arrow shows whether the answer is higher or lower.
  • Main accord and accords: how close the overall scent profile is.
  • Perfumer: green when the same nose made both.
  • Notes: how many notes your guess shares with the answer. Shared notes get a ✓ in the pyramid.

Get it right and the bottle comes into focus. Run out of guesses and it's revealed anyway, with a link to the perfume on Fragrantica.

Difficulty

  • Easy: the 150 best-known fragrances, all notes and main accords shown, a lightly blurred bottle, 10 guesses.
  • Medium: the top 300, a heavily blurred bottle, half the notes hidden at first, 8 guesses.
  • Hard: all 500, no bottle photo, two notes to start, 6 guesses.

Each missed guess uncovers another note. You can also spend hints on the decade, country, perfumer, brand initial or main accords.

Men's, women's and unisex

Use the filter to play only men's, women's or unisex fragrances, or all of them. Each combination has its own daily bottle.

Which perfumes are in the game?

The 500 most-voted fragrances on Fragrantica, from classics like Chanel No 5, Shalimar and Cool Water to modern favourites like Sauvage, Baccarat Rouge 540, Aventus and Black Opium. Designer and niche houses are both included.

Sharing

After a round, press "Copy result" to share a spoiler-free grid of your guesses.
