# Privacy Policy

How Ante handles your data. Last updated 31 October 2026. In short: everything stays on your phone, and Ante has no server.

## Who is responsible

Péter Rácz, Kálti Márk u. 2/B, Hungary. Email: peter.racz.rhr@gmail.com, phone: +36 50 104 5007.

## What stays on your phone

Your limits, the time left and used per app, your daily history (about six months), your table results, your settings and your theme. This is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

## App updates

Ante checks the project's public GitHub page (github.com) for a newer version of its screens, at most every ten minutes while you use it, and downloads it if there is one. These requests carry no personal data, but GitHub, like any website, sees your IP address and keeps standard logs under its own privacy policy (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies). Each update is signed, and Ante refuses anything that isn't.

## Which app is in front

With the accessibility permission on, Android tells Ante the name of the app that comes to the front, so Ante can cover apps that are over their limit. Ante does not read what is on your screen, what you type or your notifications. The app names are used on the phone and not stored beyond your usage totals.

## Google sign-in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Google shows its own sign-in screen and gives Ante your name and email address, which are saved on your phone. Google processes this sign-in under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Ante does not keep the Google sign-in token. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw by signing out.

## The app list

To let you pick apps, Ante reads the list of apps installed on your phone and their icons. This list stays on the phone.

## Web demo

The web demo runs in your browser with sample data and stores its state in your browser's local storage. The host (Netlify or Vercel) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

## Ko-fi

The Ko-fi link opens ko-fi.com in your browser. Anything you do there is handled by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy.

## How long

On-device data stays until you uninstall Ante or clear its data in Android settings. Daily history is limited to about six months. Signing out deletes your name and email right away.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Because the data is on your phone, you can delete it yourself at any time. For anything else write to peter.racz.rhr@gmail.com.

## Complaints

You can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), Falk Miksa utca 9–11, 1055 Budapest, naih.hu.

## Children

Ante is not meant for children under 16.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are shown in the app.
