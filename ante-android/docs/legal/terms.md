# Terms of Service

These terms apply when you use the Ante app and its web demo. Last updated 31 October 2026.

## 1. What Ante is

Ante is a screen-time tool. You set daily time limits for apps on your phone. When a limit is used up, Ante covers that app. The tables (roulette, blackjack, slots, poker and coin flip) let you stake minutes of that time budget.

## 2. No real money, no prizes

Ante is not a gambling service. You cannot pay money into the tables, and you cannot win money, goods or anything else of value. Minutes won or lost only change the time limits you set yourself, and they reset every day. Every table returns a little less than it takes on average, so over time playing reduces your screen time.

## 3. Who can use it

You need to be at least 16 years old. If gambling-style games are a problem for you, or might become one, please don't use the tables. If gambling, even with time, starts to feel like a problem, talk to your doctor or a support service near you.

## 4. Your phone and its settings

Blocking needs Android's accessibility permission, which you can turn on and off in Settings at any time. Ante can't guarantee that every app is blocked in every situation, for example web versions of apps or picture-in-picture video. Strict mode adds a 30-second wait before Settings and uninstalling; it is meant as friction, not as a lock you can't undo.

## 5. Your account

Signing in with Google is optional. It only puts your name and email on this phone. You can sign out at any time in Profile.
